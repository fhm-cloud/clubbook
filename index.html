Baseline for the book

These are the conditions the stock distances were measured in. Every change is relative to them.

Calibrate with one shot

Hit one reference shot and enter the distance. The book scales by the same ratio. Temperature and elevation are already included, so what remains is your form.

Import from Map My Bag

Paste one club per line, or choose a Trackman Performance Studio CSV. Two numbers are carry and total. One number is carry, and the club’s rollout stays. Several shots with the same club collapse into one distance.

Map My Bag numbers are normalized to sea level. Keep the baseline elevation at 0 so today’s elevation correction lands in the right place.

Which club?

Pressure and temperature change air density. The correction uses the standard atmosphere and applies 65% of that density change to carry (about +2% per 300 m, about +2% per 10 °C). Rollout stays fixed, so conditions change carry only. A locked club skips the day-form factor and still gets weather and elevation. An uncertain club is quieter in the list and loses a club search to a confident one within about 3 yards (2.5 m). Everything stays on this device.
